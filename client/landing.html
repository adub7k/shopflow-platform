The problem

Leads aren't the bottleneck. Callbacks are.

Most shops we talk to already get leads. Google, Facebook, referrals, the phone. The leads come in. Then they die.

Not because anyone is lazy. Because you can't tint a car and work the phone at the same time. Nobody can.

So the call goes to voicemail. The Facebook lead sits until tonight. The text gets answered Tuesday. By then they booked with the shop down the street.

The case study

One shop. Thirty days. Every number.

An independent window tint, ceramic coating and PPF shop in Albuquerque with three installers. They do the work. They didn't have time to run ads or chase leads, so we did both. Aug 18 to Sep 16, 2026.

We put $1,352.97 into Facebook, Instagram and Google. That brought in 276 leads, about $4.90 each. We reached 261 of them, quoted from the shop's price list, and put 54 jobs on the calendar, each one with a deposit down.

That's $25,375 of booked work for $25.06 a job. Eighteen of those jobs were done and paid inside the window, $8,062 collected at a $448 average ticket, with 31 more still scheduled. Not a testimonial. The ledger.
