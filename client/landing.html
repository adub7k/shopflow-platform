Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Is there really a free trial? +
Yes — 14 days free on every plan, no contract. Set up your shop in about two minutes and cancel anytime.
Does the AI Call Screening replace my receptionist? +
No. It's a call screening & lead capture system, not a full receptionist. It screens spam from real callers, captures missed and unanswered calls, texts the caller back instantly, and creates a CRM lead — so a real conversation can still happen and no lead is lost. It doesn't pretend to be a person or fully answer every call for you.
Which industries is ShopFlow for? +
Cleaning companies, detail shops, barbershops, nail salons and tint shops are first-class — and pressure washing and other service businesses are supported too. The whole app relabels itself to your trade.
How long does setup take? +
About two minutes: pick your business type, add your team, and connect your booking link. No training required, and white-glove setup is included on the Shop plan.
Do I need to download an app? +
No. ShopFlow runs in any browser and installs to your home screen like an app. Your customers book from a link you text them — they never download anything.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees — month to month.
