Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Will this actually work for my shop?
If you win work over the phone and live off repeat customers — tint, detail, ceramic, PPF, wraps, or any service trade — this was built for exactly your workflow. The platform reshapes itself to your trade's services and language. On the call we'll walk through it configured for your kind of shop, not a generic version.
How hard is setup?
You don't set it up — we do. Services, prices, booking calendar, phone number, automations: configured for you during onboarding. Most shops are live within days, and the receptionist starts answering from day one.
Can my employees use it? Do I need to be technical?
If your team can use a phone, they can use ShopFlow. It runs in any browser, installs to a home screen like an app, and the daily flow is simple: check the queue, tap to reply, mark jobs done. No training courses, no IT person.
I already have a CRM. Can I switch?
Yes — bring your customer list and we'll import it during onboarding, so your history comes with you. Nothing starts from zero.
What does the AI receptionist actually do?
It answers your line 24/7 in a natural voice, answers questions, quotes your real prices, books appointments on your schedule, and texts callers back. When a call needs you, it transfers live. Every call is logged in your CRM with a summary and transcript — nothing disappears into voicemail.
What does it cost?
Prices are public: the Receptionist is $349/month, the Growth Engine is $3,000/month, and Market Leader is $5,000/month — see the pricing section for what's in each. If we run ads for you, your ad budget is paid directly to Meta and is separate — every dollar of spend goes toward reaching customers, not to us. And the Growth Engine carries the 90-day promise: if the tracked revenue it recovers and books doesn't beat what you paid, month four is free.
What support do I get?
A direct line to the team that built it — not a ticket queue. Onboarding is white-glove, and you're in regular strategy meetings with us, reviewing real numbers and adjusting together.
