Leads aren't the bottleneck. Callbacks are.

Most shops we talk to already get leads. Google, Facebook, referrals, the phone. The leads come in. Then they die.

Not because anyone is lazy. Because you can't lay film and work the phone at the same time. Nobody can. So the call goes to voicemail, the Facebook lead sits until tonight, and by then they've booked with the shop down the street.

Of the 276 leads above, we reached 261. Fifteen never picked up after repeated attempts. That's the number we work on, not "engagement."

  • The ad lead sits in an inbox

  • "I'll call them back tonight"

    Tonight you're tired. Tomorrow there's a car in the bay. The lead was worth a $448 ticket. It never got a call.
