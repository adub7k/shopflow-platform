Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Is ad spend included in the price? +
No — your monthly package covers the management, creative and software. Your Meta ad budget is separate and paid directly to Meta, so every dollar of spend goes to reaching customers, not to us. We'll recommend a starting budget on your call.
Do the packages build on each other? +
Yes. Starter is the foundation (receptionist, CRM, booking, reviews). Build My Brand adds short-form content and social on top. Scale is the complete engine — it turns on Meta ad management with full content output and reporting. Every tier includes everything in the one before it.
What does the AI receptionist actually do? +
It answers your calls 24/7 in a natural voice, answers common questions, gives quotes, books appointments, and captures the lead into your CRM with a summary and transcript. When a call needs you, it transfers to a human. It's how you stop losing jobs to missed and after-hours calls.
Which businesses is this for? +
Local service businesses that rely on the phone and the calendar — detail shops, cleaning companies, barbershops, nail salons and tint shops especially. If you win jobs by phone and repeat customers, this is built for you.
How fast will I see results? +
The receptionist and CRM start capturing leads on day one. Meta ads typically take a few weeks to gather data and hit their stride — that's why we work in bi-weekly strategy cycles, reviewing the numbers and adjusting together.
Do I have to switch off my current tools? +
We handle setup for you and route everything through your ShopFlow CRM so your leads, calls, bookings and reviews live in one place. We'll sort out the details of your current setup on the onboarding call.
