The Product
What you're selling and why it's different
📱 What is ShopFlow?
ShopFlow is a CRM built specifically for barbershops. Not a generic booking tool. Not an app that's also for nail salons and dog groomers. Built for barbers, by someone in the business.

It handles: online booking, client profiles, automated SMS reminders, rebook nudges, revenue tracking, loyalty rewards, and deposit collection.

Barbers add it to their home screen. It works like a native app. No App Store download required.

How to Sell It
The process that works
🎯 The Opening DM
Keep it short. Never pitch in the first message. Just open a conversation.
"Hey bro, quick question — how's [Booksy / Square / whatever] working out for you? Asking because I work with a barbershop software and trying to see what people actually need. No pitch, just curious."
🔎 Discovery Questions
Once they engage, ask before you pitch. Let them tell you the pain.
"What are you using right now to take bookings?"
"What's that running you a month, all in with the fees?"
"How many no-shows you dealing with in a week?"
"If you could change one thing about how you run the chair, what'd it be?"
💬 When They Respond
Listen first. Then connect their pain to what ShopFlow fixes.
1
They say fees are too high → "Yeah that's exactly why this was built. ShopFlow is $20/mo, zero transaction fees."
2
They say clients don't book online → "Ours works like texting them a link. No app download. They pick a time and go."
3
They say they use paper/texts → "How much time do you spend chasing no-shows? ShopFlow auto-texts them 24hrs before."
4
They're interested → "Let me show you a live demo — 15 minutes, I'll walk you through everything. What's a good time?"
🖥️ Running the Demo
Log in to the demo account and walk them through it in this order:
1
Dashboard — show revenue, today's appointments. Tap a client name and show the full profile popping up.
2
Client profile — show visit history, cut notes, spending trend, loyalty points. "All of this builds automatically."
3
Booking page — show /book/kings-cuts. Show how a client books. Takes 30 seconds.
4
Messages tab — show the SMS inbox. "When a client texts back about their appointment, you see it right here."
5
Close — "Setup takes 2 minutes. First 14 days free. $19.99/mo after that, no contracts." Then stop talking.
🤝 The Ask for Referrals
Every closed shop knows 5 other barbers. Ask the same day they sign up — that's when they're most excited.
"Appreciate you bro. Real quick — you know any other barbers who'd want this? Send 'em my way, I'll take care of 'em."
Objection Handlers
Every objection you'll hear and exactly what to say
"I already use Booksy / Square"
"How much are you paying per month including transaction fees? Most shops save $50-200/mo switching to ShopFlow just on fees alone — and get a better product."
"My clients won't use an app"
"It's not an app — it's a link. You text it to them, they tap it, they pick a time. No download, no account. Simpler than Instagram."
"I don't have time to learn new software"
"Setup is 2 minutes. Add your barbers, share the booking link. That's it. Most barbers are taking bookings the same day they sign up."
"It's too expensive"
"It's $19.99 a month. One cut pays for a whole month. And if it brings back even one no-show client, it's already paid for itself."
"I need to think about it"
"Totally fair. I'll send you the demo link so you can look around on your own. First 14 days are free so there's no risk. Can I follow up Thursday?"
"What if I want to cancel?"
"No contract, cancel anytime. We keep it because it works, not because we locked you in."
"Send me some info" (the brush-off)
"For sure — but honestly a 10-minute demo will show you more than any PDF. You free now or later today? I'll keep it quick."
